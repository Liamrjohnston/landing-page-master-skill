# Credits

Landing Page Master is original writing. No other skill's text is copied into it. These sources shaped what it checks. Naming them is not an endorsement, and their licenses cover their own work.

## Public skills we read

- [Anthropic frontend-design](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/tree/main/skills/frontend-design): a deliberate visual direction instead of default AI styling. Apache-2.0.
- [Corey Haines marketing skills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills): copywriting, conversion, form and signup workflows. MIT.
- [jezweb Claude skills](https://github.com/jezweb/claude-skills), [OneWave AI Claude skills](https://github.com/OneWave-AI/claude-skills), [mbfinotti advertising skills](https://github.com/mbfinotti/advertising-skills) and [kostja94 Pagina](https://github.com/kostja94/pagina): building, honest copy and audit ideas.
- [Vercel web interface guidelines](https://github.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines), [Addy Osmani web quality skills](https://github.com/addyosmani/web-quality-skills) and [agent skills](https://github.com/addyosmani/agent-skills): interface, accessibility and performance review.
- [wondelai skills](https://github.com/wondelai/skills), [taste-skill](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill), [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable), [MengTo skills](https://github.com/MengTo/Skills) and [UI UX Pro Max](https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill): design and conversion material. Unsafe installers, made-up numbers and fake-proof patterns were left out.

## Books and experts

Most of these were read through public talks, samples and author sites, not the full books.

- Karl Blanks and Ben Jesson, Making Websites Win (the authors' free audiobook): find the barrier before you redesign, answer the real objections.
- Donald Miller, Building a StoryBrand: a clear customer, outcome and next step.
- April Dunford, Obviously Awesome: position against the buyer's real alternatives.
- Eugene Schwartz, Breakthrough Advertising (through secondary sources): match what the visitor already knows.
- Joanna Wiebe, Copyhackers: use the customer's own words.
- Steve Krug, Don't Make Me Think: make the action obvious.
- Adam Wathan and Steve Schoger, Refactoring UI: hierarchy, spacing and a consistent visual system.
- Luke Wroblewski, Mobile First and Web Form Design: phone-first layouts and short forms.
- Chip and Dan Heath, Made to Stick, and Drew Eric Whitman, Cashvertising (secondary coverage): no unsupported persuasion stats were used.
